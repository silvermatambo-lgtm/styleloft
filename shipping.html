Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery arrangements, fees and special courier options for Style Loft Boutique.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

ShippingPre-OrdersReturnsPrivacyTerms

1. Delivery coverage

Style Loft Boutique delivers within South Africa. International delivery may be considered, depending on destination, product eligibility and courier availability. An international shipping quotation will be provided before dispatch. Customs duties, import taxes and other destination charges may be payable by the customer unless expressly stated otherwise.

2. Courier services

Our preferred courier is The Courier Guy, where available and appropriate. PEP stores and PUDO lockers may be considered where our preferred courier is unavailable. We will verify suitable alternatives for the delivery area.

3. High-risk addresses

Couriers may flag certain addresses for security reasons. If an additional courier fee is required, the customer may choose to pay it before delivery or request a suitable supported collection point such as a PUDO locker. Available options and charges will be communicated before dispatch.

4. Standard South African delivery fees

Package sizeDelivery fee
Small packageR120

Delivery fees are separate from product prices, and the applicable fee depends on the order size and nature. Fees may be reviewed if courier or operating costs change. Final charges should be confirmed before paid checkout.

5. Pre-order delivery

Pre-ordered pieces have an estimated arrival window of 3–6 weeks from the close of the pre-order window or deposit confirmation. Timing may change because of production, international transport or customs clearance. Significant delays will be communicated by email.

Pre-order items are dispatched after any required outstanding balance has been settled through the store's secure payment process. See the Pre-Order Policy for details.
