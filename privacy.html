Privacy Policy

How Style Loft handles customer and browsing information.

Last updated: 25 October 2026 (draft supplied by Style Loft)

About this redesign: This page describes Style Loft's supplied privacy-policy intentions. The GitHub/Vercel redesign is a static browsing preview; live purchases, account registrations and payment processing take place on the separate existing store.

1. Information collected

The store may collect your name, email, telephone number, delivery address, account details and technical data such as IP address, browser information and essential cookies, depending on the service you use.

2. Purposes of use

Customer information may be used to fulfil orders, provide delivery tracking, answer questions and process returns. Promotional marketing communications should be sent only when you actively opt in, with an opportunity to unsubscribe.

3. Service providers

Necessary contact and delivery information may be shared with couriers such as The Courier Guy, PUDO or PostNet. Payments on the existing store may be handled by third-party payment gateways such as PayFast, Yoco or Paystack, subject to the payment methods actually enabled there. Hosting and technical partners may handle limited information required to operate and protect the store.

4. Safeguarding information

Style Loft's supplied policy describes access restrictions and security measures for handling customer details. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely risk-free. Keep account credentials private, and do not submit card details or passwords into the preview enquiry forms.

5. Your privacy rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request information about data held about you, ask for correction or deletion where applicable, and object to certain processing including direct marketing. Contact the store to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies

The existing store may use cookies for shopping baskets and site analytics. Disabling cookies may affect features. The redesign preview also uses device-local storage for the demo wishlist and preview bag; this does not submit an order to the store.

7. Contact

Privacy enquiries: info@styleloft.co.za. The merchant should confirm its designated Information Officer and production data-handling details before presenting this page as a verified compliance statement.

These customer policy pages reflect the document supplied by Style Loft Boutique. The redesign remains an enquiry preview; purchase-specific checkout terms and mandatory South African consumer protections govern actual orders.
