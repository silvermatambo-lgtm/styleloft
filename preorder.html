Love it? Secure it on pre-order

Some highly anticipated pieces may be reserved before arrival. For eligible items, the original-store pre-order arrangement described in the supplied policy requires a 50% deposit to reserve the piece, followed by the remaining 50% when the item is ready for shipment.

1. Paying the balance

When an item reaches the warehouse and is ready to ship, Style Loft will email a secure payment link for the outstanding 50%. The supplied draft requests payment of the balance within 7 days; any cancellation, deposit treatment and rights under applicable law must be explained at the actual pre-order checkout.

2. Delivery timing and delays

Estimated arrival is 3–6 weeks, calculated from the date the pre-order window closes or the deposit is confirmed. Production schedules, international courier timelines and customs can cause delays. Style Loft will provide updates when significant delays occur.

3. Cancellations and refunds

Pre-orders may be subject to different conditions from items already in stock. The supplied draft includes specific deposit and supplier-cancellation conditions that require merchant review before they can be described as a final customer promise. Any final terms remain subject to mandatory consumer rights.

4. Mixed orders

When in-stock items and pre-order items are placed in the same order, the draft policy provides for dispatch together once the pre-order item arrives. For earlier dispatch of in-stock items, place them as a separate order.

Questions?

Contact info@styleloft.co.za for pre-order availability and verified payment instructions.
