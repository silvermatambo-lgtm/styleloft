Returns & Exchanges

Guidance on returns, exchanges, defects and refunds.

Last updated: 25 October 2026 (draft supplied by Style Loft)

ShippingPre-OrdersReturnsPrivacyTerms

1. Return and exchange windows

For standard returns or size exchanges, please submit a request within 14 days of delivery. The supplied policy also recognises a period of up to 6 months for defective or damaged goods under the South African Consumer Protection Act, subject to applicable statutory rights and remedies.

2. Item condition

  • Clothing and bags: normally unworn, unwashed, unaltered and free from marks or strong fragrances, with original tags attached.
  • Shoes: try on only on carpeted surfaces and keep the original, undamaged shoe box inside a protective courier bag or box.
  • Hygiene-related items: the supplied draft identifies bodysuits, swimwear, undergarments and earrings as ordinarily excluded from change-of-mind returns. Statutory remedies for defective goods remain available.

3. Return courier costs

For non-defective size exchanges and change-of-mind returns, the supplied draft makes the customer responsible for return shipping and for courier fees on a replacement item. If an item arrives defective, damaged or incorrect, Style Loft's draft provides that the store covers return courier costs, subject to applicable consumer law.

4. Request a return

  1. Email info@styleloft.co.za with your order number in the subject.
  2. Describe whether you seek an exchange, store credit or refund. Include photos for defects.
  3. Once you receive return instructions and an address, use a trackable courier service. Please do not return parcels without first contacting the store.

5. Inspection and refund timing

The supplied draft allows 3–5 business days for inspection once a parcel is received. Where a refund is approved, it is normally processed to the original payment method, with a further indicative 5–10 business days for bank or gateway processing. Actual timing depends on the payment provider.

6. Promotions and original shipping

The draft proposes store credit or size exchange for some clearance items and restrictions on refunding original shipping charges. Those provisions apply only to the extent permitted by law and cannot take away remedies for defective goods.

7. Contact
